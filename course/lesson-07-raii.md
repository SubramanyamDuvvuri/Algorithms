# Lesson 7 - Constructors, Destructors, RAII, and Special Members

## Objective

Master constructors, destructors, raii, and special members well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

Construction order, invariants, destructors, RAII, Rule of Zero/Three/Five, move-only owners, and partial construction.

## Engineering Lab

Build file, socket, and mapped-file owners. Add failure injection to a three-resource transactional bundle and prove rollback.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Explain every generated, deleted, defaulted, or handwritten special member.
