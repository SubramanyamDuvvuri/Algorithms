# Lesson 10 - Smart Pointers and Ownership Design

## Objective

Master smart pointers and ownership design well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

`unique_ptr`, custom deleters, `shared_ptr` control blocks and aliasing, `weak_ptr`, cycles, and explicit ownership semantics.

## Engineering Lab

Build an ownership graph visualizer and async connection manager; demonstrate and repair a shared-pointer cycle and shutdown resurrection bug.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Justify every shared owner and every observer.
