# Lesson 9 - Operators, Value Semantics, and Conversions

## Objective

Master operators, value semantics, and conversions well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

Operator design, hidden friends, regular values, `<=>`, `explicit`, conversion operators, strict weak ordering, and conversion traps.

## Engineering Lab

Implement checked `Money`, `Price`, `Quantity`, and fixed-point decimal types with parsing, formatting, comparison, hashing, and property tests.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Prove comparison/hash consistency and absence of accidental conversions.
