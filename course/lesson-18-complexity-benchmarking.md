# Lesson 18 - Complexity, Benchmarking, and Measurement

## Objective

Master complexity, benchmarking, and measurement well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

Big-O, time/space complexity, amortized analysis, workload distributions, `chrono`, benchmark hygiene, percentiles, and noise.

## Engineering Lab

Build a reusable harness; measure vector growth, hashing, and tree traversal with median/p95/p99 and environment metadata.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Find confounders and support every performance claim with evidence.
