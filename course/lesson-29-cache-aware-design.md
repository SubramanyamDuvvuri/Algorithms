# Lesson 29 - Caches and Data-Oriented Design

## Objective

Master caches and data-oriented design well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

CPU caches, cache lines, AoS/SoA, branch prediction, likely/unlikely, misses, false sharing, hot/cold splitting, and measurement.

## Engineering Lab

Benchmark AoS/SoA, branchy/reduced filters, and padded/contended counters; redesign the order-book hot path from access patterns.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Tie every claimed gain to representative measurements and hardware behavior.
