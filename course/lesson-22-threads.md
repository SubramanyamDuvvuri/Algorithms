# Lesson 22 - Threads, Lifetime, and Data Races

## Objective

Master threads, lifetime, and data races well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

`thread`, `jthread`, start/join/detach, argument passing, stop tokens, thread lifetime, data races, and interleavings.

## Engineering Lab

Demonstrate a real race and repair it three ways; build a cancellable periodic worker and audit cross-thread callback captures.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Name every shared object and its lifetime/synchronization relation.
