# Lesson 20 - Graphs and Graph Algorithms

## Objective

Master graphs and graph algorithms well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

Representations, BFS, DFS, topological sort, Dijkstra, A*, Union-Find, minimum spanning trees, and path reconstruction.

## Engineering Lab

Build a generic graph library, dependency resolver, A* route planner, and Kruskal/Prim implementations with differential tests.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Prove preconditions, correctness, and why Dijkstra rejects negative edges.
