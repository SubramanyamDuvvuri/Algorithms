# Lesson 4 - Enums and Type-Safe Flags

## Objective

Master enums and type-safe flags well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

Traditional enums, `enum class`, underlying types, scope, conversions, exhaustive switches, and valid bit patterns.

## Engineering Lab

Implement a constrained `FlagSet<E>` with validation and unknown-bit round-tripping for protocol evolution.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Defend type safety, representation, and compatibility behavior.
