# Lesson 35 - ECS and Data-Oriented Architecture

## Objective

Master ecs and data-oriented architecture well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

Entities, generations, components, systems, sparse sets, archetypes, query planning, structural mutation, scheduling, and cache-friendly iteration.

## Engineering Lab

Build stale-handle-safe ECS with sparse storage, queries, deferred mutation, and parallel scheduling; benchmark sparse-set versus archetype workloads.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Defend identity, layout, query cost, mutation rules, and race prevention.
