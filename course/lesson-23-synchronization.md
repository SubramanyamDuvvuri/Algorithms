# Lesson 23 - Mutexes, Deadlocks, and Coordination

## Objective

Master mutexes, deadlocks, and coordination well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

`mutex`, guards, `unique_lock`, `scoped_lock`, four deadlock conditions, condition variables, producer/consumer, latches, barriers, and semaphores.

## Engineering Lab

Implement a closeable bounded MPMC queue with timed operations; stress shutdown, lost-wakeup, saturation, and lock-order cases.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

State protected invariants, predicates, lock order, and shutdown transitions.
