# Lesson 14 - Template Foundations

## Objective

Master template foundations well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

Function/class templates, deduction, dependent names, specialization, non-type parameters, `auto`, CTAD, and instantiation.

## Engineering Lab

Implement generic `SmallVector<T,N>`, heap, and graph types; test move-only and throwing elements.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Trace overload selection and the exact point of instantiation.
