# Lesson 30 - Allocators and Memory Resources

## Objective

Master allocators and memory resources well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

Allocation costs, arena/bump/pool/stack allocators, alignment, propagation, polymorphic allocators, `std::pmr`, and `memory_resource`.

## Engineering Lab

Implement bump and pool allocators; make trie/event batches PMR-aware; measure count, footprint, fragmentation, and latency.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Defend resource ownership, escape prevention, lifetime, and OOM behavior.
