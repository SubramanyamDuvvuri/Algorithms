# Lesson 26 - Futures, Promises, Async, and Cancellation

## Objective

Master futures, promises, async, and cancellation well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

`async`, launch policy, promise/future/shared_future, packaged tasks, continuations, deadlines, cancellation, and structured async ownership.

## Engineering Lab

Build a bounded executor returning futures and a fan-out/fan-in query with deadline budgeting and exception propagation.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

For each task: where it runs, who owns/waits/stops it, and how it fails.
