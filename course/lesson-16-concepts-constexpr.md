# Lesson 16 - Concepts and Compile-Time Programming

## Objective

Master concepts and compile-time programming well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

C++20 concepts, requires expressions, constraints/subsumption, `constexpr`, `consteval`, `constinit`, and compile-time computation.

## Engineering Lab

Constrain the Lesson 14 types; build a `consteval` schema validator and unit-safe dimensional arithmetic.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Distinguish syntactic constraints from semantic laws.
