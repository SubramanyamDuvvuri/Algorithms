# Lesson 12 - Iterators, Algorithms, Ranges, and Views

## Objective

Master iterators, algorithms, ranges, and views well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

Iterator categories, `<algorithm>`, C++20 ranges, projections, sentinels, lazy views, borrowing, and pipeline composition.

## Engineering Lab

Replace manual loops with algorithms; build a zero-copy log pipeline and a conforming custom chunk view.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Explain execution timing, ownership, complexity, and invalidation of each pipeline.
