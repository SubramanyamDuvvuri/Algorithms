# Lesson 34 - Dynamic Libraries, ABI Stability, and Plugins

## Objective

Master dynamic libraries, abi stability, and plugins well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

Dynamic loading, visibility, mangling, calling conventions, versioned C interfaces, opaque handles, registries, discovery, hot reload, and compatibility.

## Engineering Lab

Build a host and two plugins with capability/version negotiation; add reload generation tracking and prevent unload with live code/objects.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Defend ownership, allocators, exceptions, versions, and unload safety across the ABI.
