# Lesson 31 - SIMD, Auto-Vectorization, and Parallel STL

## Objective

Master simd, auto-vectorization, and parallel stl well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

SIMD fundamentals, intrinsics, compiler vectorization, execution policies, `reduce` versus `accumulate`, associativity, and reproducibility.

## Engineering Lab

Compare scalar, transformed, auto-vectorized, intrinsic, and parallel kernels; inspect vectorization reports and numeric differences.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

Explain why each loop vectorizes or fails and what semantic freedom parallel reduction uses.
