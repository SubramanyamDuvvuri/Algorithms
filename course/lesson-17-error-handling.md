# Lesson 17 - Errors, Exceptions, `expected`, and Guarantees

## Objective

Master errors, exceptions, `expected`, and guarantees well enough to implement, test, review, benchmark, and explain production code.

## Theory

Exceptions, unwinding, basic/strong/no-throw guarantees, `noexcept`, error codes, `std::expected`, context, and propagation.

## Engineering Lab

Implement one parser with exceptions and expected; inject allocation/I/O failures and prove post-failure state.

## Required Evidence

Submit working C++20 code, focused tests, invariant and complexity notes, failure analysis, benchmark evidence where performance is relevant, and a short design review recording rejected alternatives.

## Mastery Gate

State failure modes, channel, guarantee, and retry validity for every API.
